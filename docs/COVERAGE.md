## Known gaps

- This is not a complete world register. It contains 32,748 directory records from the sources listed below; other universities and education organisations are certainly missing.
- Source-checked programme, fee, deadline, scholarship and recognition facts exist for 2 of 25,290 university-scope records (under 0.1%). The rest are discovery records only.
- Directory inclusion is not university status, accreditation or degree-granting authority. 7,458 education records are kept in the broader “education” scope because they are neither in the Hipo university directory nor named like a university.
- “University scope” combines the community Hipo university directory with a multilingual name heuristic for ROR records. It can include organisations that are not universities and can miss universities with unusual names.
- Hipo is a community-maintained directory, not an official register. It may contain non-university institutions and may be out of date. Its entries are labelled as community directory listings.
- No currency conversion is applied in this release. Fees are shown in their original currency and cannot be compared across currencies without a dated exchange-rate source.
- Recognition and accreditation are shown only where an official university or authority page was read. Absence of a recognition entry is not evidence that an institution is unaccredited (1 record currently have one or more entries).
- Cities are missing for 2,574 records. No city is inferred from a domain, a postal address or another institution.
- 8,734 distinct city strings are recorded; these are source spellings, not a normalised world city list.
- Programme catalogues are selective samples of official pages. A missing programme is not a finding that it is not offered, and a listed programme does not guarantee admission.
- Former names are included only when a source names them. Local-script and alternate names come from the directory sources and are not complete.
- Deadline dates can change and are shown with the source's own wording and the date the page was read. UniScope does not monitor pages continuously.
- The subject classification is UniScope’s own grouping, not a national, accreditation or publisher taxonomy; 2 of the taxonomy’s subject areas currently have at least one programme fact.
- No ranking is produced. Publisher rankings are not mixed, and no overall quality, personal-fit or admission-chance score is calculated.
